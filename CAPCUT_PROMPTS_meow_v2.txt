1) ANA MÜZİK PROMPTU (20-25 saniyelik cinematic teaser score)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dark cinematic crypto trailer score, mysterious and premium, building
tension from quiet dread to an epic payoff. Deep sub-bass drones, slow
rising string ostinato, distant metallic percussion hits, soft digital
glitch textures, no vocals. Should feel like a AAA game reveal trailer
or a high-budget movie teaser — never cheesy, never generic EDM drop.

TIMELINE:
0.0–3.0s   — near silence, single low sub-bass pulse, distant heartbeat-like
             kick every ~1s, faint high-frequency shimmer (unease, not yet scary)
3.0–7.0s   — soft rising string/pad ostinato enters, subtle ticking/clock
             percussion layered in, tension building but restrained
7.0–11.0s  — a second layer joins (low brass swell or distorted synth),
             rhythm accelerates slightly, occasional glitch/stutter FX
11.0–14.0s — full crescendo, all layers driving, a hard rhythmic pulse
14.0–14.8s — hard cut to near-total silence (just a faint low rumble) —
             this is the "held breath" moment before the reveal
15.0–15.6s — single massive cinematic bass hit / impact (sub-drop +
             metallic braam), everything else silent around it
15.6–19.0s — triumphant but restrained swell (warm pad + soft choir-like
             tone), confident not celebratory, still mysterious
19.0–20.0s (or end) — fade out, one last soft low tone lingering

Mood keywords: dark, premium, mysterious, cinematic, suspenseful,
restrained, confident, AAA trailer, no vocals, no cheesy synth leads.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
2) ALTERNATİF MÜZİK PROMPTU ("Uyuyan Dev" / gizemli-epik versiyon)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ancient, geological, slow-building epic score — think a giant waking
underground. Very low sustained drone as a foundation, distant deep
rock-cracking rhythmic hits (not literal foley, musical/tonal), a slow
melodic motif on low strings or duduk-like woodwind entering around the
midpoint, building to a single deep tectonic "impact" moment, then a
short triumphant swell that stays dark/minor-key, never major/happy.
No vocals, no traditional drum kit — texture and drone driven.
